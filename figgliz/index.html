Turn five minutes of break into an unexpected encounter

Figgliz is a Chrome extension for discreet one-to-one conversations with randomly matched people: text chat, short voice notes, optional webcam sessions and lightweight games, without public profiles or social pressure.

View plans

Figgliz is currently in beta testing with volunteer users before its public Chrome Web Store release.

Built for spontaneous conversations, not public performance

Figgliz keeps the interaction direct, light and intentionally minimal: no public feed, no follower count, no profile theatre.

Random one-to-one matching

Start a conversation with someone new while keeping identity and public presence out of the experience.

Text, voice and webcam

Chat by text, send short voice notes, or open a webcam session only after an explicit invitation is accepted.

Games as icebreakers

Invite someone to chess, checkers, Connect 4, Ping Pong, Flappy Duo or Air Hockey when words need a warm-up.

Safety controls first

Use Next, block, report, availability controls and warnings before risky contact-information sharing.

Games that make the first minute easier

Game invitations are designed as small social bridges: play only, or play while keeping a webcam session open when both people agree.
