Turn five minutes of break into an unexpected encounter

Figgliz is a Chrome extension for discreet one-to-one conversations with randomly matched people: text chat, short voice notes, optional webcam sessions and lightweight games, without public profiles or social pressure.

Figgliz is currently in beta testing with volunteer users before its public Chrome Web Store release.

About Figgliz

Figgliz is a Chrome extension for anonymous conversations, with optional voice notes, video, webcam backgrounds, games, and a discreet launcher.

Fast anonymous chat

Become available, get matched with someone compatible, and keep essential controls close: Next, block, report, voice notes, video, and availability.

Text, voice, and video

Text remains the core experience. Voice notes and video start only from explicit actions, with visible controls to accept, decline, mute, or stop.

More playful webcam

In fullscreen video, Plus and Pro members can use blur and video backgrounds, with mirror, camera pause, draggable previews, and clean controls.

Games and video

Ping-Pong, Air Hockey, Flappy Duo, and other games can be played with or without webcam. Game sessions currently stay P2P to preserve server stability.

Discreet launcher

The floating shortcut opens the side panel, shows unread messages, can collapse into a tab, and can be disabled in settings.

Built-in safety

The personal contact detector warns before sensitive details are sent. Reports, blocks, quotas, action tokens, and license checks reduce abuse.

Arcawand Soft

Figgliz is developed by Arcawand Soft to make anonymous chatting more alive, controlled, and comfortable than a classic anonymous chat.

Conversation history

Plus and Pro members can open a local history of meaningful conversations, including messages, voice notes, animated emojis, and accepted events such as calls, webcams, or games. This is not a server archive: it stays on the User's device, is protected by a password, and can be deleted conversation by conversation or entirely.

Free calls and gifted credits

Free includes 1 accepted call per day, video or audio, whether incoming or outgoing. Plus members can gift 2 call credits per day to a Free peer who has used the daily call. Monthly or yearly Pro members can gift 5 per day, and the Pro Lifetime launch offer can gift 10 per day.

Games that make the first minute easier

Game invitations are designed as small social bridges: play only, or play while keeping a webcam session open when both people agree.
