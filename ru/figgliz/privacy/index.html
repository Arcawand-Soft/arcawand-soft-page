Privacy policy

Privacy PolicyFiggliz

Figgliz limits data collection and explains what stays local, what goes through the VPS, and what is used for safety.

Local data

The extension may locally store language, theme, launcher state, preferences, anonymous ID, blocks, usage counters, and license state.

VPS server data

The VPS processes presence, queueing, matching, statuses, WebRTC signaling, quotas, action tokens, license verification, reports, and anti-abuse protections.

Conversations

Messages and voice notes use WebRTC DataChannel when possible. The application server is not designed as a conversation history database.

Camera and microphone

Chrome asks for permission before any camera or microphone access. You can stop video, mute, pause camera, or revoke permission.

Video backgrounds and segmentation

Blur and webcam backgrounds are applied locally with bundled resources. They compose your video stream; they are not used to identify your room.

Games

Games exchange technical actions and states between the two users. VPS game hosting is currently disabled to preserve server stability.

Licenses and payments

Dodo Payments handles payment. The extension and server verify the exact product, status, period, and rights needed, without storing full card details.

Reports and moderation

A report may include reason, comment, anonymous IDs, IP hashes, metadata, and elements strictly useful for moderation.

No sale

Figgliz does not sell personal data, messages, voice notes, or videos, and does not use them for targeted advertising.

Your controls

You can become unavailable, edit settings, hide the launcher, block, report, remove the local license, manage your plan, and revoke camera/microphone.

Contact

Support, privacy, safety, or billing: contact@arcawand-soft.com.

Local history and user control

When history is enabled, eligible conversation items are kept locally by the extension on the User's device. The User stays in control: a single conversation or the entire history can be deleted from the dedicated interface.

Password and no server access

History is protected by a local password and secret question. The password is not intended to be sent to the server for remote reading. If it is forgotten or reset, full deletion of the local history is required to prevent unauthorized access.

Quota and credit verification

To enforce Free limits, gifted credits and Plus/Pro rights, Figgliz may verify on the server the license status, local installation ID, IP address or hash, time zone needed for daily counters, action tokens and acceptance events. These checks prevent cheating and do not mean server-side storage of conversation content.
